# Clipforge Studio: Support

Clipforge Studio is a video editor and converter for iPhone, iPad and Android. Every edit, conversion and export
runs on your device: your videos are never uploaded.

## Contact

Email: **resulcayop@gmail.com**

We answer in English, usually within a few working days. When you report a problem, please include:

- the app version (Settings › About › Version),
- your device model and its iOS or Android version,
- what you did, what you expected and what happened instead,
- if you can, the app's logs: Settings › Storage &amp; diagnostics › Diagnostics › Export logs. They hold file names
  and technical details, never your media. Look them over before you send them; sending them is up to you.

Please don't send videos unless we ask for one; a description of the file (format, length, which app or camera
made it) is usually enough.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where do my exports go?**
Finished files are listed in the Exports tab. From there, use Save to Photos (MP4, MOV and pictures), Save to
folder, or Share. On iPhone and iPad, exports are also in the Files app under On My iPhone/iPad › Clipforge ›
Exports.

**Why is "Save to Photos" missing for some files?**
Photos only accepts formats it can play, such as MP4 and MOV. Other formats (for example WebM, MKV or audio
files) can be saved to a folder or shared.

**A video won't play in the editor.**
Some formats can't be played by the phone's built-in player. Tap "Create playable preview": Clipforge makes a
lighter copy for previewing. Your export still uses the original file.

**Exports are slow or the file is larger than expected.**
Make sure Settings › Performance › Hardware encoding is on. Without it, H.264 and HEVC can't be written and videos
are saved as MPEG-4, which is larger and slower. Clipforge also slows down while the device is hot or in a
power-saving mode.

**Why can't I choose HEVC (H.265)?**
HEVC is only offered when your device has a hardware HEVC encoder. Clipforge tests what your device supports the
first time it starts; you can see the result in Settings › Media engine.

**Clipforge is taking a lot of storage.**
Settings › Storage &amp; diagnostics › Storage shows what the app uses. "Clear caches" removes preview copies,
thumbnails and waveforms; they are made again when needed and no project is affected.

**An export failed or was interrupted.**
Open it in the Exports tab and tap Retry. Interrupted jobs (for example, when the system closed the app) can be
retried too. If it keeps failing, the error details tell you why (often low free space); please send us the logs
if it isn't clear.

**Can I see the tour again?**
Settings › How Clipforge works.

**Does Clipforge need an account or an internet connection?**
No. There are no accounts, ads or analytics. The only data that can leave the device is a crash report (no media, file names or text you typed)
if the app crashes; you can switch that off in Settings › Storage &amp; diagnostics › Diagnostics › Send crash reports.
